Detection Architecture & Severity Model

Combining deterministic rule engines with statistical NLP classifiers and passive forensic analyzers for low false-positive tolerance.

Detection & Decision Pipeline
  • Deterministic Rule Engine: High-precision keyword and pattern matching for severe profanity, physical violence threats, and credential theft triggers.
  • Statistical NLP Classification: TF-IDF feature extraction with calibrated Machine Learning models for subtle semantic aggression.
  • Explainable AI (XAI): Generates human-interpretable evidence summaries linking triggered indicators directly to risk categories.
  • Passive Forensic Inspection: Safe URL analysis without outbound connection risks, and memory-only static attachment header parsing.
Severity Classification Tiers
CRITICAL RISK Physical violence threats, executable malware payloads
HIGH RISK Compounding harassment, credential phishing traps
MEDIUM RISK Single targeted insult, suspicious URL shorteners
LOW / CLEAN Legitimate academic and administrative communications

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

BullyMail processes email headers, text snippets, and uploaded security telemetry submitted for cyberbullying and threat intelligence analysis. User authentication records include username, institutional email address, and role assignments.

2. Data Usage & Confidentiality

Submitted email telemetry is processed strictly for risk scoring, threat classification, and explainable evidence extraction. We do not sell, rent, or distribute ingested data to third-party advertisers.

3. Security Controls & Encryption

All stored operational credentials and mail server credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256 (Fernet master keys). Session cookies are protected via HttpOnly and SameSite attributes.
