Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

BullyMail processes email headers, text snippets, and uploaded security telemetry submitted for cyberbullying and threat intelligence analysis. User authentication records include username, institutional email address, and role assignments.

2. Data Usage & Confidentiality

Submitted email telemetry is processed strictly for risk scoring, threat classification, and explainable evidence extraction. We do not sell, rent, or distribute ingested data to third-party advertisers.

3. Security Controls & Encryption

All stored operational credentials and mail server credentials are encrypted at rest using AES-128-CBC + HMAC-SHA256 (Fernet master keys). Session cookies are protected via HttpOnly and SameSite attributes.
